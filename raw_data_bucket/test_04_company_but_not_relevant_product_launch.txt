MobileFirst Games Launches "Dragon Quest Legends" Mobile Game

Los Angeles, CA - December 19, 2025

MobileFirst Games, an independent mobile game studio, announced today the global launch of "Dragon Quest Legends," their latest free-to-play RPG for iOS and Android devices.

The game features stunning 3D graphics, over 200 unique characters to collect, and a deep storyline spanning 50+ hours of gameplay. Players can team up with friends in cooperative raids or compete in PvP arenas for exclusive rewards.

"Dragon Quest Legends represents two years of passionate development by our talented team," said Michael Park, Creative Director at MobileFirst Games. "We've created something truly special that we think RPG fans will love."

The game includes innovative gameplay mechanics like real-time combat, a dynamic weather system that affects battles, and seasonal events with limited-time characters and rewards.

Key Features:
- 200+ collectible heroes with unique abilities
- Real-time multiplayer battles
- Stunning graphics optimized for mobile
- Regular content updates with new storylines
- Cross-platform progression between iOS and Android

The game is available now as a free download on the App Store and Google Play. Optional in-app purchases are available for players who want to accelerate their progress.

MobileFirst Games has also partnered with popular streamers and content creators to promote the launch, with tournaments and giveaways planned throughout the month.

"We're committed to supporting Dragon Quest Legends with fresh content for years to come," Park added. "This is just the beginning of the adventure."

Early reviews from gaming media have been positive, with GameSpot giving it an 8/10 and calling it "a refreshing take on mobile RPGs."

Download Dragon Quest Legends today and begin your epic journey!
