ShopEasy and PaymentPro Announce Strategic Marketing Partnership

New York, NY - December 16, 2025

ShopEasy, an e-commerce platform for small businesses, today announced a strategic marketing partnership with PaymentPro, a payment processing company.

Under the partnership, PaymentPro will become a preferred payment partner featured prominently in ShopEasy's merchant onboarding flow. In return, PaymentPro will promote ShopEasy to its existing merchant base through co-branded marketing campaigns.

"This partnership allows us to offer our merchants a seamless payment solution right out of the box," said Rachel Thompson, VP of Partnerships at ShopEasy. "PaymentPro's technology integrates perfectly with our platform."

The companies will run joint webinars, create co-branded marketing materials, and exhibit together at industry trade shows throughout 2026. They're also planning a joint customer referral program offering discounts to businesses that use both platforms.

"ShopEasy has an excellent reputation among small business owners," said Mark Johnson, Chief Marketing Officer at PaymentPro. "This partnership helps us reach that audience more effectively."

Neither company disclosed financial terms of the partnership, but both emphasized it is primarily a marketing and go-to-market collaboration rather than a technology integration.

The partnership kicks off in January 2026 with a joint booth at the National Retail Federation's Big Show in New York City.

###

About ShopEasy:
ShopEasy provides e-commerce platform solutions for small and medium businesses. Founded in 2019, the company is headquartered in New York, NY.

About PaymentPro:
PaymentPro offers payment processing services for online and brick-and-mortar businesses. The company was founded in 2015 and is based in Austin, TX.
