Acme Cloud Inc. Raises $50M Series B

December 15, 2025 Funding

San Francisco, CA - Acme Cloud Inc., a leading provider of cloud infrastructure solutions, announced today that it has raised $50 million in Series B funding led by Venture Capital Partners with participation from existing investors TechFund and Innovation Capital.

The company plans to use the funds to accelerate product development and expand into the European market. CEO Sarah Chen stated, "This funding will enable us to scale our operations and bring our innovative cloud management platform to enterprises worldwide."

Acme Cloud has experienced rapid growth over the past year, tripling its customer base to over 500 enterprise clients. The company's flagship product, CloudControl Pro, has been recognized as a leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Cloud Management Platforms.

In addition to the funding announcement, sources close to the company revealed that Acme Cloud is in advanced negotiations to acquire a smaller competitor, StreamOps Technologies, which would significantly expand their DevOps automation capabilities. The acquisition is expected to close in Q1 2026.

Industry analysts note that this positions Acme Cloud as a strong contender in the rapidly consolidating cloud infrastructure market.
