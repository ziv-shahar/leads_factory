TechFlow Inc. - Major Corporate Restructuring Timeline

March 15, 2025 - FUNDING ANNOUNCEMENT
TechFlow Inc. announced the closure of a $120 million Series D funding round led by Tiger Global Management and Sequoia Capital. The round brings the company's total funding to $250 million since its founding in 2018. CEO Maria Rodriguez stated, "This capital will accelerate our product roadmap and international expansion."

June 8, 2025 - ACQUISITION COMPLETED
TechFlow Inc. completed the acquisition of DataPipe Solutions, a Toronto-based analytics startup, for $45 million. The deal brings 35 DataPipe employees into TechFlow's engineering team. "DataPipe's technology perfectly complements our platform," said Rodriguez. The DataPipe Toronto office will be integrated into TechFlow's operations by Q4 2025.

August 22, 2025 - WORKFORCE REDUCTION
In a surprising move, TechFlow Inc. announced layoffs affecting 18% of its workforce (approximately 150 employees), primarily in sales and marketing divisions. The company cited "strategic realignment to focus on enterprise customers" as the reason. CFO James Mitchell confirmed that the company is consolidating three regional offices into two larger hubs to "optimize operations and reduce overhead costs."

September 30, 2025 - OFFICE CONSOLIDATION UPDATE
TechFlow has signed a new lease for 65,000 square feet in downtown Austin, Texas, consolidating employees from its existing 40,000 sq ft Austin office and the soon-to-close 35,000 sq ft Dallas office. The move is expected to complete by Q1 2026. Approximately 85 Dallas-based employees will either relocate to Austin or transition to remote work.

---
This timeline demonstrates how growth, acquisition, and restructuring can happen simultaneously in high-growth companies.
