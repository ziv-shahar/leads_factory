GreenTech Solutions Named "Best Sustainability Software" by EcoTech Awards

Portland, OR - December 17, 2025

GreenTech Solutions, a sustainability software platform, has been recognized with the prestigious "Best Sustainability Software 2025" award by the EcoTech Awards, an industry organization honoring innovation in environmental technology.

The award recognizes GreenTech's comprehensive carbon tracking and ESG reporting platform, which helps companies measure, track, and reduce their environmental impact.

"We're honored to receive this recognition from EcoTech," said Jennifer Martinez, CEO of GreenTech Solutions. "Our mission has always been to make sustainability measurable and actionable for businesses of all sizes."

GreenTech's platform has been adopted by over 1,000 companies worldwide to track carbon emissions, generate ESG reports, and identify opportunities for environmental improvement. The software integrates with existing business systems and provides real-time dashboards for sustainability metrics.

The EcoTech Awards jury praised GreenTech for its user-friendly interface, comprehensive reporting capabilities, and positive impact on corporate sustainability practices. "GreenTech Solutions exemplifies the kind of innovation needed to address climate challenges," said award jury chair Dr. Robert Chen.

This is the second major award for GreenTech this year. In March, the company was also named to Fast Company's list of "Most Innovative Companies in Sustainability."

GreenTech Solutions was founded in 2018 and is based in Portland, Oregon. The company's platform is used by organizations ranging from small businesses to Fortune 500 companies.

For more information about GreenTech Solutions and their sustainability platform, visit www.greentechsolutions.com.
