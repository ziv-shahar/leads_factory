CyberShield Expands to Three New Markets, Opens Offices in London, Singapore, and Toronto

Austin, TX - December 22, 2025

CyberShield, a rapidly growing cybersecurity software company, announced today it is expanding internationally with three new offices opening in Q1 2026 to support surging global demand for its threat detection platform.

The company will establish regional headquarters in London (UK), Singapore, and Toronto (Canada), with plans to hire approximately 200 employees across the three locations over the next 18 months.

"We're seeing explosive growth in EMEA, APAC, and Canadian markets," said Jennifer Lee, CEO of CyberShield. "Opening local offices allows us to better serve our international customers and tap into world-class talent pools in each region."

The expansion comes on the heels of CyberShield's successful $85M Series B funding round in September 2025, led by Accel Partners. The company has grown from 150 employees at the start of 2025 to 380 today, with plans to reach 650 employees by end of 2026.

Details of the expansion:

London Office:
- 25,000 square foot space in Shoreditch tech hub
- Initial team of 60 people focusing on sales, customer success, and engineering
- Targeting businesses across UK and EMEA region
- Office opening: February 2026

Singapore Office:
- 18,000 square foot office in Marina Bay Financial Centre
- Team of 50 covering APAC sales, support, and regional partnerships
- Focus on expanding in Southeast Asia, Australia, and Japan markets
- Office opening: March 2026

Toronto Office:
- 20,000 square foot space in downtown financial district
- 90-person team for Canada and Latin America markets
- Will include engineering team working on compliance for Canadian privacy regulations
- Office opening: January 2026

"All three locations offer access to exceptional cybersecurity talent," said Mark Peterson, Chief People Officer at CyberShield. "We're actively recruiting for roles across engineering, sales, customer success, and operations in each market."

The company's Austin headquarters will also undergo expansion, with CyberShield signing a lease for an additional 30,000 square feet adjacent to its current 50,000 square foot office to accommodate continued growth in the US market.

CyberShield's platform protects over 2,500 enterprise customers from cyber threats, with annual recurring revenue growing 350% year-over-year. The company competes with established players like CrowdStrike and SentinelOne in the cloud-native security market.

"This international expansion is a critical milestone in CyberShield's journey to becoming the global leader in threat detection," Lee added.

The company is hosting virtual recruiting events in all three new markets throughout January 2026. Interested candidates can visit careers.cybershield.com for open positions.
